One single-line review of the most boring everyday object: the rubber band in the junk drawer, dried out years ago, that still snaps in half the one time it is actually reached for.

> The rubber band in my junk drawer dried out years ago, yet it still snaps in half the one time I actually reach for it.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the review to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 200 characters of UTF-8 text, a single line.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
